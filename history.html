Our History

From a living room meeting in 1983 to a thriving congregation with 15+ ministries — this is the story of New Jerusalem Baptist Church.

Where It Began

Born Out of
Community Need

As the minority population began to increase in the East and West Lynwood Additions at the end of the 1970s, there was one church in the East Lynwood Addition — but no predominantly minority church to serve the new residents moving into West Lynwood. After much prayer and divine leadership from the Holy Spirit, a survey confirmed the need for a new worship facility in the area.

The Lord guided the founders to the home of the late Sister Rena Mae Jett on Central Freeway, who graciously opened her doors for the organizational meeting. On September 7, 1983, New Jerusalem Baptist Church was born.

Timeline

Milestones of Faith

★
September 7, 1983
New Jerusalem Baptist Church Is Founded
After much prayer and community outreach, the organizational meeting was held in the home of Sister Rena Mae Jett on Central Freeway, Wichita Falls. A congregation was born out of a genuine need and a calling from God.
⛪
Fall 1983
First Worship Services — East Lynwood Community Center
The earliest worship services were held at the East Lynwood Community Center, bringing together the founding congregation for prayer, song, and the preaching of God's Word.
🏘
October 1, 1983
Moving to Borton Lane
Three weeks after founding, services were relocated to 1400 Borton Lane — the neighborhood that would become the permanent home of New Jerusalem Baptist Church.
🔨
Decades of Growth
Building and Expanding
Through hard work, perseverance, and faithful giving, the congregation enlarged the original small frame building over the years, adding space to accommodate a growing church family and its expanding ministries.
🏗
September 2002
New Sanctuary Construction Begins
With the congregation outgrowing the original structure, construction began on a new sanctuary — a testament to the faithfulness of the members and the provision of God.
✝
Easter Sunday 2003
First Service in the New Sanctuary
On the most joyful Sunday of the Christian calendar, New Jerusalem held its first worship service in the new sanctuary — a moment of celebration and dedication to God's glory.
▶
October 2025
Rev. Anthony Phillips Called as Senior Pastor
New Jerusalem extended the call to Rev. Anthony Phillips to serve as Senior Pastor, beginning a new chapter of biblical conviction, Spirit-led vision, and community engagement for the congregation.
🌟
Today
A Living, Growing Congregation
More than 40 years after its founding, New Jerusalem Baptist Church remains a beacon of hope in Wichita Falls — loving people, serving the community, and pointing hearts toward Jesus Christ.
In Loving Memory

Honoring Our Founding Pastor

✝
Founding Pastor · In Loving Memory

Pastor Angus B. Thompson

New Jerusalem Baptist Church was built on the foundation laid by our beloved Founding Pastor, Angus B. Thompson. His vision, faith, and leadership shaped this congregation from its very first meeting in 1983. His legacy lives on in every ministry, every soul reached, and every life transformed by the Gospel at New Jerusalem. We honor his memory and continue the work he began.
