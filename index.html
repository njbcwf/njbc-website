A Church That
Shows Christ's Love

The mission and purpose of New Jerusalem Baptist Church is to carry out the mandate of Jesus — to "feed His sheep." We are a church that shows the love of Christ to everyone we meet. Every member is seeking fellowship with GOD, growing together in faith, and serving our community in Wichita Falls.

"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost."

— Matthew 28:19
