Who We Are

New Jerusalem Baptist Church is a community of faith rooted in Wichita Falls, Texas — committed to loving people and letting God do the saving.

✝ New Jerusalem Baptist Church
Our Mission

Carrying Out the
Mandate of Jesus

The mission and purpose of New Jerusalem Baptist Church is to carry out the mandate of Jesus — to "feed His sheep." We believe in loving every person we encounter and trusting God to transform hearts. Every ministry, every service, every act of service flows from this calling.

"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you."

— Matthew 28:19–20
What We Believe

Our Core Values

✝
Christ-Centered
Jesus is the foundation of everything we do — our worship, our outreach, and our community life.
💛
Radical Welcome
We believe every person deserves to feel seen, loved, and at home — no matter where they come from.
📖
Biblical Truth
Scripture is our guide. We teach the Word faithfully and apply it to everyday life.

Community Service
Faith without works is incomplete. We actively serve the Wichita Falls community through our ministries.

Fellowship
We do life together — celebrating victories, carrying burdens, and growing as one body in Christ.
Our Story

A History of Faith

For decades, New Jerusalem Baptist Church has been a pillar of the Wichita Falls community — growing, adapting, and always staying rooted in the Gospel.

EST
Founding
New Jerusalem is Established
A small group of believers gathered with a shared vision: to build a church that truly loved its community and carried the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
✝
Growth
Expanding Our Ministries
As the congregation grew, so did our outreach. Ministries were formed to serve youth, seniors, families, and the broader Wichita Falls community.
🏘
Community
Borton Street Home
We settled at 1420 Borton St — a home base from which we continue to serve, worship, and welcome all who come through our doors.
▶
Today
Growing in Faith & Reach
With 15+ active ministries, weekly worship services, and an online presence reaching beyond Wichita Falls, NJBC continues to love people and let God save them.
