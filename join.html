Getting Connected

How to Get Plugged In

Joining the NJBC family is simple. Here are four easy steps to get connected and start growing.

1
Visit on a Sunday
Join us for Sunday School at 9:30 AM or Morning Worship at 11:00 AM. Our greeters will welcome you at the door — no appointment needed.
2
Meet the Team
After service, introduce yourself to our greeters or any minister. We'd love to meet you, answer questions, and help you find your place here.
3
Explore a Ministry
Browse our 15+ active ministries and find one that matches your gifts and passions — from youth to seniors, worship to community outreach.
4
Join the Family
When you're ready, express your desire to become a member. Our pastoral team will walk you through what membership at NJBC means and looks like.
